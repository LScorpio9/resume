Research Projects

STAT4 Genetic Analysis in Autoimmune Disease

Conducted large-scale genetic meta-analysis investigating STAT4 variants in autoimmune diseases. Integrated GWAS datasets, SNP annotation, linkage disequilibrium analysis, and eQTL resources including GTEx and immune-cell-specific regulatory databases.

Single-cell RNA-seq Analysis of Immune Cells

Performed single-cell transcriptomic analysis of more than 200,000 immune cells using Seurat, Harmony, and Azimuth. Developed workflows for quality control, doublet removal, batch correction, cell-type annotation, and differential expression analysis.

CRISPR Screening Analysis

Analyzed genome-scale CRISPR screening data including guide RNA abundance, dropout evaluation, and functional gene screening analysis.
