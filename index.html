PhD Candidate | Computational Biology | Immunology

I am a PhD candidate specializing in computational biology, genomics, and immunology. My research focuses on integrating single-cell transcriptomics, statistical genetics, and functional genomics to understand immune mechanisms underlying autoimmune diseases.

Research Interests

  • Single-cell RNA sequencing and immune cell profiling
  • Computational immunology
  • Statistical genetics and GWAS meta-analysis
  • Autoimmune disease mechanisms
  • CRISPR functional screening
  • Multi-omics integration

Research Projects

STAT4 Genetic Analysis in Autoimmune Disease

Large-scale GWAS meta-analysis and functional annotation of STAT4 variants in autoimmune diseases using genetic association, LD analysis, and eQTL resources.

Single-cell RNA-seq Analysis of Immune Cells

Analysis of large-scale single-cell transcriptomic datasets using Seurat, Harmony, and Azimuth for immune cell profiling and multi-sample integration.

CRISPR Screening Analysis

Computational analysis of genome-scale CRISPR screening data, including guide RNA abundance, dropout evaluation, and functional gene identification.
