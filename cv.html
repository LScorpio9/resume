Research Profile

I am a PhD candidate specializing in computational biology, immunology, and genomics. My research focuses on integrating single-cell transcriptomics, statistical genetics, and functional genomics to investigate immune mechanisms underlying autoimmune diseases.

Research Experience

Computational Immunology and Single-cell Transcriptomics

  • Analyzed large-scale single-cell RNA sequencing datasets involving more than 200,000 immune cells.
  • Developed workflows using Seurat, Harmony, Azimuth, and scDblFinder for quality control, batch correction, and cell-type annotation.
  • Investigated immune cell heterogeneity and molecular signatures associated with autoimmune diseases.

STAT4 Genetic Analysis in Autoimmune Diseases

  • Performed GWAS meta-analysis investigating STAT4 genetic variants across autoimmune diseases.
  • Integrated SNP annotation, linkage disequilibrium analysis, and immune-cell-specific eQTL resources.
  • Evaluated regulatory effects of disease-associated variants using functional genomics approaches.

CRISPR Functional Screening Analysis

  • Analyzed genome-scale CRISPR screening datasets.
  • Evaluated guide RNA abundance, dropout patterns, and screening quality.
  • Developed computational pipelines for functional gene discovery.
